Our Mission

We're solving the disconnect between people and their communities.

Millions of tax dollars maintain outdoor spaces that go underutilized while screen time climbs and mental health declines. We saw a gap — and built a bridge.

Connectify is a community-centered platform designed to make exploring your local world as engaging as scrolling your feed. Through personalized recommendations, gamified exploration, and locally focused event boards, we're turning passive screen time into real-world connection.

Our approach is simple: make going outside more fun, more social, and more rewarding than staying in. When communities thrive, everyone benefits.

Our Values

The principles that guide everything we build

Every feature, every decision, every line of code is shaped by what we believe matters most.

Community First

Every feature we build starts with one question: does this bring people closer together? We design for neighborhoods, not networks.

Explore Boldly

We encourage curiosity and discovery. The best experiences are waiting just around the corner — and we make finding them effortless.

Trust & Safety

Real connections require real trust. We build safety into every interaction so communities can grow with confidence.

Joy in the Journey

Gamification isn't a gimmick — it's our way of making real life more rewarding. Every outing becomes an adventure worth sharing.

Locally Rooted

Global scale means nothing without local impact. We're built for the places you live, the parks you pass, and the people next door.

Awareness Driven

We don't just connect — we inform. From daily gems to local event boards, we help communities know what's happening around them.
